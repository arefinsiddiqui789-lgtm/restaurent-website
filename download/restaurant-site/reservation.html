Good to Know

How Reservations Work

We hold tables for 15 minutes past the reservation time. If you're running late, a quick call goes a long way — we'll do our best to hold your spot.

Walk-ins are always welcome at the bar, where we serve the full menu plus a small bites list. Bar seats are first-come, first-served.
